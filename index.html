M.S. candidate in Financial Engineering, Columbia University

I am a master’s student in financial engineering at Columbia University. I am currently studying how AI’s physical, energy, and human footprints show in prices, economic growth, and ethical meaning.

Before Columbia, I spent two years in energy & infrastructure investment banking at DC Advisory, where I was part of the first analyst cohort in the NY team. Prior to that, I studied economics, philosophy, and physics at the University of Chicago. There I wrote honors theses in two fields: one with Esteban Rossi-Hansberg on the pricing of renewable energy certificates, and one with Robert Pippin on art as a ground of normativity. I completed my high school education at Choate Rosemary Hall, where I graduated cum laude and was editor-in-chief of the school newspaper.

Most of what I am currently interested in sits at the intersection of physics, philosophy, and economics.

I also run MINNÉ Fragrances, a niche fragrance brand I built myself from the ground up. It started as a passion project to explore my artistic side, but is now somewhat of a full business. You can find it at minnefragrances.com and Instagram.
